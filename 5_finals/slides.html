Our recommendation

Approve the 90-day retention pilot: NT$1.1M expected, inside NT$2.5M, for postpaid customers who tell support they may leave.
  • Week 1: connect the weekly notes file; first call list to the call centre
  • Day 45: stop paid offers if contacted customers leave as often as held-back ones
  • Day 90: proven if the gap is 3.1 percentage points or more
  • Then: scale retention to the full base; redirect roaming and broad upsell spend
